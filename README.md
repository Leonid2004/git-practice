# On AI and Security

## The article I found interesting

[OpenAI boss says world 'right to be afraid' but 'should trust' AI firms](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cqx2zpj4y525o)

I think this article is very interesting and important especially these days. AI has gotten extremely advanced in a wide range of fields. While many people are using AI for various purposes, it is important to start asking questions about the safety of AI before it is too late.

This article is about who gets to decide what is safe and what is not. OpenAI says the world should trust them to do the right thing, and the CEO of NVIDIA, Jensen Huang, says safety is an engineering problem, not a legal one.
